Privacy + Terms · Beta v2026-08-25

Know what’s private before you start.

Second Chance is built around one simple rule: your personal work should stay yours unless you choose to share it.

Plain-English versionYour private journal answers are not shown to the creator by default. The creator can see progress, Life Scores, action receipts, return/activity patterns, feedback you intentionally submit, chat messages you intentionally send, reflections you explicitly share, and private coaching notes the creator keeps for follow-up.

What Second Chance stores

Account: email address, account identifiers, sign-in information, and basic account activity.
Journey: completed steps, current step, pace, Life Scores, action receipts, return/activity information, drafts, and reflections.
Chat: messages you intentionally send to Marquise, timestamps, read status, and the step/section you were on when you opened chat when available.
Feedback + bug reports: feedback you intentionally submit. A bug report may include the screen, step, section, browser/device information, and the description you type.
Creator notes: the creator may keep private coaching notes based on your progress or conversations. These notes are for creator follow-up and are not your journal entries.

Your journal privacy

Your reflection text is private by default. The creator does not get access to private reflection text just because you completed a step.

If you intentionally use a share option, paste something into chat, or submit it through a clearly labeled shared-feedback area, that specific information becomes visible to the creator.

Second Chance does not sell participant journal content or use private reflections for advertising.

Chat expectations

You can message Marquise anytime through the in-app chat. The normal response target is within 8–12 hours, but that is a target rather than a guaranteed emergency response time.

Second Chance chat is not emergency or crisis support. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Reminders

If you choose “Need a nudge?”, Second Chance stores the reminder time you picked. Browser reminders depend on the permissions and capabilities of your device. You can cancel or replace a reminder at any time.

How creator access works

The creator dashboard is designed to show what is useful for coaching and improving the program without opening private journals. It may show signup/onboarding progress, completed-step counts, Life Scores, action receipts, returns after a break, submitted feedback, chats, deliberately shared reflections, and creator-only notes.

Beta product + security

Second Chance is currently a beta product. Features may change, and occasional bugs or interruptions can happen. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can promise perfect security or uninterrupted availability.

Do not use Second Chance as the only place to keep information you cannot afford to lose.

Account and data requests

During the beta, you can ask for account/data help or deletion through the in-app chat with the creator. Some records may need to be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Using Second Chance

Use the program for lawful personal-growth purposes. Do not attempt to access another person’s account, interfere with the service, upload malicious content, or use the service to threaten or harm others.

Second Chance provides guided personal-growth content and creator support. It is not a substitute for licensed medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or emergency services.
